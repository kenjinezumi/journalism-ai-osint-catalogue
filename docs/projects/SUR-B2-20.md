## 1. Purpose

Searchable directory of Citizen Lab's published research on targeted surveillance, censorship, spyware vendors and information controls, with full reports and technical indicators.

## Journalism / investigative use case

The reference corpus for surveillance-industry reporting: before investigating a vendor, read what Citizen Lab has already established about its infrastructure, customers and targeting patterns — which saves duplicating forensic work and provides citable attribution that a newsroom usually cannot produce itself.

## 2. Audience

journalist, newsroom analyst, specialist investigator

## 3. Project type

dataset · bucket `proprietary-service`

## 4. Pros

- The most authoritative open body of research on commercial spyware and its deployment against journalists, with technical depth no newsroom can replicate in-house
- Reports include infrastructure indicators and methodology, so they are usable as both background and a starting point for your own verification
- Citable academic provenance, which materially strengthens a published claim about a vendor's conduct
- Free and publicly accessible, with the directory verified live in this survey

## 5. Cons

- Not a tool or a dataset you can query programmatically — it is a publication archive, so systematic use means reading rather than analysis
- Coverage follows Citizen Lab's research priorities, which are extensive but not comprehensive: absence of a vendor from the directory means nothing about that vendor
- Findings are point-in-time: a vendor's infrastructure and customer list change after publication, so an indicator from a 2023 report may be stale
- Reproducing or extending their technical findings requires forensic capability (MVT-class work) that most newsrooms will need a partner for

## 6. Feedback (documented)

No individual user reports verified in this survey. Documented: the publication directory was verified live on 2026-11-17 (200). Citizen Lab's research is extensively cited in journalism and litigation concerning commercial spyware, which is the relevant evidence of its standing.

## 7. Assessment

Treat it as required reading rather than tooling — and as the route to a partnership. For your profile the highest-value action is not consuming the reports but building a relationship: Citizen Lab, Amnesty Security Lab and Access Now have forensic capability your newsroom will not, and surveillance stories involving journalist targeting typically require exactly that. Read the directory to know what is established, then bring them what you find.

## 8. Maintenance

**Actively maintained** — Directory verified live on 2026-11-17 (200). An actively publishing academic research group; maintenance means continued publication rather than software releases, and the research output is continuous and public.
