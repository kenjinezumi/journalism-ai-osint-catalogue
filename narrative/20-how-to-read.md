## How to read this

- **Scores** apply the rubric in `sources/recovered/cat/RUBRIC.md` unchanged: relevance 30,
  capability 20, maintenance 15, privacy and deployment control 15, usability 10, licence and
  cost 10. Each dimension is 0-5; the weighted total is out of 100.
- **The ranking encodes my priorities for your work**, not universal software quality. A
  specialist tool scored against its intended role will sit below a ubiquitous one even when it
  is excellent at its job — ExifTool ranks first because it is used on nearly every verification
  and never fails, not because it is more sophisticated than QGIS.
- **Evidence confidence is recorded separately from the score** and never folded into it. 251
  projects are high confidence, 97 medium, 13 low; 23 are marked provisional.
- **Material blockers are listed separately from scores** so a licence or dependency problem is
  visible even where the tool scores well.
- Assessment depth is stated per project. **Nothing in this survey was installed or run.** Depth
  is `metadata-verified`, `repo-inspected` or `docs-reviewed`; no entry claims hands-on testing.
- Project claims, documented user feedback and my own judgement are separated in every card.
  Where no user reports were found, the card says so rather than inventing consensus.
